SKIMMING - Definition

Skimming is a reading technique used to get the general idea or overall meaning of a text quickly. When we skim a text, we do not read every single word carefully. Instead, we focus on the most important parts, such as the title, headings, first sentences, last sentences, keywords, and repeated ideas.

Example: Imagine you receive a 5-page article about environmental problems. Your teacher asks, "What is this article mainly about?" You don't need to read every sentence—just read the title, headings, and key sentences.

How to use Skimming:

  • 1. Read the title.
  • 2. Look at headings & subheadings.
  • 3. Read the 1st sentence of each paragraph.
  • 4. Look for repeated keywords.
  • 5. Pay attention to the conclusion.
  • 6. Decide the general topic of the text.
Soal

SKIMMING - Example 1

Passage:

Sarah's English teacher gave the students a four-page article entitled "Healthy Lifestyle for Teenagers." The article discussed several topics, including healthy eating, regular exercise, getting enough sleep, drinking enough water, and managing stress.

Sarah only had three minutes before the class discussion started. Instead of reading every sentence carefully, she first looked at the title. Then, she read the headings and the first sentences of several paragraphs. She noticed that the article repeatedly discussed ways teenagers could improve their physical and mental health. After doing this, Sarah was able to explain that the article was generally about healthy habits that teenagers should develop in their daily lives.

Question:

Which reading technique did Sarah use?

A. Scanning
B. Reading for Detail
C. Inference
D. Skimming
E. Vocabulary in Context
Pembahasan

Pembahasan: SKIMMING - Example 1

✅ Jawaban: D. Skimming

Alasan: Jawabannya adalah D. Skimming karena Sarah hanya melihat judul (title), subjudul (headings), dan kalimat pertama dari setiap paragraf. Ia tidak membaca seluruh artikel kata demi kata. Tujuannya adalah untuk memahami topik umum artikel secara cepat.

Tindakan khas Skimming yang dilakukan Sarah dalam teks:

  • Looked at the title
  • Read the headings
  • Read the first sentences
  • Understood that the article was generally about healthy habits
